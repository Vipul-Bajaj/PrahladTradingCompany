<p><strong>Description</strong></p>
<p>When movement is constant and safety can't pause, the PN231(A) is your go-to restraint companion. This Y-shaped adjustable rope lanyard, crafted from Ø11mm kernmantle rope, ensures uninterrupted hand protection during high-mobility tasks. Its polyamide core integrates a wear-detecting color tracer and abrasion-resistant thimbles at all critical ends. A Steel Karabiner provides primary anchorage, while dual Aluminium Scaffold Hooks offer seamless transitions between anchor points keeping you connected at all times. Polyethylene sleeves reinforce stitching, adding durability in demanding zones.
