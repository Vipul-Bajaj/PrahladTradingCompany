<p><strong>Description</strong></p>
<p>The SLBL 10 Sealed Block offers dependable fall protection for workers operating in tough conditions like offshore platforms and chemical plants. Its sealed design safeguards the internal mechanism from dust, moisture, and contaminants, ensuring consistent performance over time. Equipped with a durable stainless steel wire rope, it delivers smooth, controlled fall arrest and vertical mobility. The unit features a swivel snap hook with a built-in load indicator and a sturdy D-shackle for reliable anchorage. Lightweight yet resilient, the SLBL 10 is a trusted choice for maintaining safety in challenging industrial environments.</p><p><strong>Warranty: </strong>6 months from the date of invoice against any manufacturing defects. For more details on the terms and conditions, <a href="https://www.karam.in/product-warranty-and-disclaimer">click here</a>.</p>
