About Prahlad Trading Company

Prahlad Trading Company is a leading distributor of industrial safety equipment and personal protective equipment (PPE) in Raipur and surrounding regions. With over 20 years of experience serving the industrial community, we have built a reputation for providing authentic, certified safety products from trusted manufacturers.

Founded and led by Bipin Agrawal, our company is an authorized dealer for numerous leading safety brands. We specialize in a comprehensive range of products including safety helmets, safety shoes, harnesses, welding products, and fire safety equipment. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner for factories, construction sites, workshops, and industrial facilities across the region.

Every product we supply is 100% authentic and meets international safety standards. We believe that safety is not just a regulatory requirement—it's a responsibility we take seriously to protect workers and contribute to safer workplaces.
